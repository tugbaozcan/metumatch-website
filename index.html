Project Objective

To design, develop, and launch an effective AI-powered digital internship and career matching platform within 3 months, at a cost not to exceed $50,000. The platform aims to successfully onboard 500 active students and 50 corporate partners within the first month of launch.

Core Deliverables

Student Portal

Profile creation, resume upload, verified GPA and course information integration.

Employer Portal

Internship and job posting dashboard with ranked candidate matching.

AI Matching Engine

Algorithm that evaluates fit based on academic data, interests, and skills.

Admin Dashboard

Career Services can track engagement, placement metrics, and employer feedback.

User Manuals

Digital onboarding documents for students, employers, and administrators.

Responsive Web App

Accessible on desktop and mobile browsers without requiring a native app.

Technical Requirements

  • Secure read-only API integration with the university SIS
  • Cloud-hosted architecture with 99.9% uptime
  • Scalable for up to 10,000 concurrent users
  • KVKK-compliant handling of student data
  • Fully mobile-responsive user interface

Project Scope Statement

The project aims to design, develop, and launch an AI-powered digital internship and career matching platform for METU within 3 months and under a $50,000 budget. The first phase targets 500 active students and 50 corporate partners in the first month after launch.


  • In Scope: Student portal, employer portal, AI matching engine, admin dashboard, and onboarding manuals.
  • Technical Scope: SIS integration, cloud hosting, KVKK compliance, and mobile-responsive access.
  • Out of Scope: Native mobile apps, financial transactions, alumni beyond the initial launch window, and direct career counseling tools.

Limits & Exclusions

  • No native iOS or Android app in Phase 1
  • No payroll, contract signing, or financial transactions
  • Only current undergraduate and graduate students included
  • No automated resume rewriting or interview coaching

Stakeholder Analysis

Power / Interest Matrix

  • Manage Closely: Director of University Career Center, University IT Department, Project Manager & Core Development Team
  • Keep Satisfied: University Administration / Rectorate, Legal & Compliance Department
  • Keep Informed: Target Students, Corporate Partners, Student Council & Career Clubs
  • Monitor: Alumni Network

Engagement Strategy

  • Weekly status updates and milestone sign-offs for primary sponsors
  • Early technical involvement from the IT Department for API and security approval
  • Beta testing with selected students and employers for direct feedback
  • Launch communication support from student clubs and campus representatives

Primary Sponsors

Director of University Career Center

Head of IT Department

Influence Network

The most critical relationship is between the Project Team and the University IT Department because secure academic data access depends on close coordination. The project also relies on two-way feedback loops with students and employers, while the Rectorate and Legal functions remain oversight stakeholders with approval power.

AI Agent Role

The AI agent supports the project by assisting with structured matching logic, campaign content generation, and selected project planning tasks such as communication support and concept development.

Social Media Campaign Concept

The campaign introduces METUMatch to the campus community through short, clear student-focused messages that emphasize easier internship discovery, verified profiles, and stronger employer connections.
